About IEDC

The Innovation and Entrepreneurship Development Centre (IEDC) at MES Keveeyam College provides students with a platform to explore innovative ideas, develop entrepreneurial skills and transform ideas into meaningful projects and ventures.

IEDC encourages students to think creatively, identify opportunities, collaborate with others and take their ideas beyond the classroom.
